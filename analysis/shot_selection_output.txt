Generated before the variable-wind (METAR VRB) fix, and wrong.

That run treated every hour with a null wind direction as CALM. A null direction is not
calm - it is the code VRB, meaning the wind was light and variable, which the standard
only permits at 6 knots or less. Roughly 35% of Central Park hours carry it. Treating
them as calm deleted real wind of 1.5-3 m/s from a third of the record.

Because wind error grows with the square of flight time, deleting that wind flattered long
shots enormously and left short ones almost untouched. Measured, for January:
